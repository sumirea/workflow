# Roadmap

> **Status:** Skeleton (structure only — full content intentionally deferred).
> Part of the Sumirea Product Blueprint (`docs/product/`).

## Purpose

Sequence the capabilities and features over time — the order, dependencies, and
milestones — so priorities are explicit and shared.

## Scope

- In: milestone sequencing, priorities, and dependencies between
  capabilities/features; "now / next / later" framing.
- Out: hard delivery dates/commitments; design of individual items (→ `../rfcs/`).

## Canonical status

**Not canonical** (evolving). The roadmap is expected to change as we learn.

## Relationship to other documents

- Orders the items in `capability-map.md` / `feature-map.md`.
- Reflects priorities set by `principles.md` and bounded by `non-goals.md`.
- Each roadmap item typically becomes an RFC in `../rfcs/` before work starts.
- Long-range arc traces back to `vision.md`.

## Outline

- Horizon framing (now / next / later — not dates)
- Milestones (each: goal · capabilities/features included · dependencies · exit
  criteria)
- Sequencing rationale (why this order)
- Link from milestones to their RFCs (once they exist)
