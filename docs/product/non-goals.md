# Non-Goals

> **Status:** Skeleton (structure only — full content intentionally deferred).
> Part of the Sumirea Product Blueprint (`docs/product/`).

## Purpose

Declare explicitly what Sumirea will **not** be or do, so scope stays disciplined
and "no" has a citable home.

## Scope

- In: out-of-scope product directions, anti-personas, categories we decline to
  become, and time-bounded "not yet" items (clearly distinguished from "never").
- Out: the positive product model (→ `capability-map.md`, `feature-map.md`);
  reasons we choose things (→ `principles.md`).

## Canonical status

**Canonical.** The authoritative guardrail against scope creep. RFCs proposing
something listed here must first amend this document.

## Relationship to other documents

- The inverse of `capability-map.md` / `feature-map.md` (what's deliberately
  absent from them).
- Bounds `identity.md` and `vision.md` (neither may claim a non-goal).
- Derived from `principles.md` (non-goals are principles applied to scope).

## Outline

- Product non-goals (never)
- Not-yet (time-bounded; with the condition that would revisit them)
- Anti-personas / who we don't build for
- Categories we explicitly are not (to prevent miscategorization)
