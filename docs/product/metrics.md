# Metrics

> **Status:** Skeleton (structure only — full content intentionally deferred).
> Part of the Sumirea Product Blueprint (`docs/product/`).

## Purpose

Define how Sumirea measures success — the North Star and supporting indicators —
so that vision and capabilities become checkable rather than aspirational.

## Scope

- In: the North Star metric, supporting/health metrics, and what each is meant
  to detect.
- Out: the qualitative story of success (→ `vision.md`); analytics
  implementation details.

## Canonical status

**Not canonical** (evolving). Metrics will be refined as the product and its
users mature.

## Relationship to other documents

- Quantifies the success described in `vision.md`.
- Tracks the health of capabilities in `capability-map.md`.
- Should respect `principles.md` and `non-goals.md` (we don't optimize for what
  we refuse to be).

## Outline

- North Star metric (definition + why it reflects real value)
- Supporting metrics (per capability / per surface)
- Guardrail / counter-metrics (what we watch so we don't game the North Star)
- Anti-metrics (numbers we deliberately do not chase)
