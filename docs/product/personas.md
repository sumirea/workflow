# Personas

> **Status:** Skeleton (structure only — full content intentionally deferred).
> Part of the Sumirea Product Blueprint (`docs/product/`).

## Purpose

Describe who Sumirea is built for — the user archetypes, their goals, contexts,
and pain points — so capability and feature decisions stay grounded in real
needs.

## Scope

- In: target user archetypes, their jobs-to-be-done, contexts, and frustrations.
- Out: anti-personas / who we don't serve (→ `non-goals.md`); how needs become
  capabilities (→ `capability-map.md`).

## Canonical status

**Not canonical** (evolving). Personas sharpen as we learn from real users.

## Relationship to other documents

- Justifies entries in `capability-map.md` (every capability should serve a
  persona's need).
- The positive complement to the anti-personas in `non-goals.md`.
- Helps `surface-map.md` decide which surfaces matter to whom.

## Outline

- Persona summary table (name · role · primary goal · key pain)
- Per-persona detail (context, jobs-to-be-done, frustrations, what success
  looks like for them)
- Persona → capability needs (traceability hint into `capability-map.md`)
