# Vision

> **Status:** Skeleton (structure only — full content intentionally deferred).
> Part of the Sumirea Product Blueprint (`docs/product/`).

## Purpose

Describe the longer-term change in the world Sumirea is trying to create — the
future state, why now, and the strategic direction — so that day-to-day choices
ladder up to something coherent.

## Scope

- In: the 3–5 year aspiration, the problem we believe is unsolved, why this
  matters now, the direction of travel.
- Out: the one-line positioning (→ `identity.md`), specific features and
  sequencing (→ `feature-map.md`, `roadmap.md`).

## Canonical status

**Not canonical** (evolving). Vision is directional and will be revised as the
market and product mature. Identity stays stable; vision adapts.

## Relationship to other documents

- Expands `identity.md` over a longer horizon.
- Provides the "why" that `principles.md` operationalizes into trade-offs.
- Sets the targets that `metrics.md` makes measurable.
- Informs the long-range arc of `roadmap.md`.

## Outline

- The world today (the gap)
- The future we're building toward
- Why now
- Strategic direction / where we will and won't lean
- Success, told as a story (qualitative; quantified in `metrics.md`)
